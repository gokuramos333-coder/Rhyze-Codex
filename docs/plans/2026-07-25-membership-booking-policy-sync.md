2. Move the $7 intro offer opening to August 3, 2026; activate its seven-day window only on the first standard-class booking.
3. Queue a one-day-before-expiration upgrade email when a trial activates and deliver queued transactional email through Resend.
4. Require current waiver acceptance during account creation, store the signed acceptance on the new account, and remove post-signup waiver prompts from booking/profile navigation.
5. Move referral code entry to eligible paid membership checkout only; exclude the intro offer and pay one $20 instructor commission after the customer’s first redeemed referral purchase.
6. Make class booking choices reflect the signed-in member’s live credit balance or unlimited access.
7. Surface unread in-app messages on member portal entry and reorganize member navigation.
