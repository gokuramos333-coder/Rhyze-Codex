These features should work together inside our current database and dashboard. Do not create a separate application, duplicate customer database, or disconnected marketing system.

The correct brand name is RHYZE FITNESS.

MAIN GOALS

1. Track personal outreach to customers who are not members.
2. Show which staff member contacted each customer, when, how, and the outcome.
3. Create a Membership Leads view using existing customer records.
4. Add an admin-only Newsletters section.
5. Create branded, customizable email templates.
6. Automatically populate newsletters with the correct classes and events.
7. Send campaigns to selected customer groups.
8. Support photo uploads, fonts, colors, and layout customization.
9. Allow immediate sending and scheduled sending.
10. Track delivery, recorded opens, repeat opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, and supported booking/membership outcomes.
11. Preserve previous campaigns, their exact sent content, and their analytics.
12. Easily duplicate past campaigns and the last weekly schedule email.
13. Highlight engaged non-members for appropriate personal follow-up.
