# Admin membership changes

Approved billing rule: keep the original renewal date; Stripe prorates the difference for the remaining period. Build locally for review, with no production deployment or customer mutation.

The client Memberships card gets a plan/date selector, then a server-calculated Stripe quote and explicit confirmation. Supported targets are active paid monthly memberships, not trials, packs, events, or single classes. Offer now, next renewal, or a future day in the current paid period. Other future dates require waiting until that billing period; do not invent recurring calendars or silently reset an anchor. Existing manual/imported access without a paid native subscription is not converted into automatic billing by a plan-name edit.

Replace the existing subscription item, never add a second subscription. Immediate changes use pending updates and immediate prorations; future changes use Stripe schedules with the same monthly interval and automatic (unchanged) anchor. Preserve discounts/settings or reject a configuration that cannot safely be preserved. Failed payments do not grant upgraded access. Negative prorations become Stripe billing credit, not a cash refund.

A durable operation records the original/new product, verified subscription/customer/item/price IDs, paid period, effective date, server quote, actor, idempotency key and execution status. One in-flight operation per membership. Double submissions, uncertain network failures and duplicate/out-of-order webhooks must not charge or grant twice. Preview is read-only at Stripe. Confirmation revalidates the source state.

Paid invoices drive plan/access activation, including scheduled changes and renewals. Preserve historical purchases and payments. The linked membership product is the current entitlement product; the original purchase remains a receipt for the original plan. Mid-period limited-plan credits equal the new allowance less net credits already consumed in that period. Retain internal overuse debt and floor only displayed/spendable availability at zero so subsequent cancellations cannot manufacture extra credits. Converted unlimited bookings get individual reservation entries for normal credit-return handling; their historical fee policy stays unchanged. At renewal grant the new full allowance once. Unlimited access requires paid-through proof. Existing booked classes are not cancelled or rewritten by this feature.

Track every invoice/payment funding the current period, including its base charge and prorated adjustments. A full refund or dispute restricts that period's access and prevents resume until billing is resolved; refunds from genuinely older paid periods do not revoke a separately paid new period. A shared advisory lock plus MVCC row version makes concurrent bookings and fulfillment safe under serializable retries. Ambiguous Stripe writes retain a durable billing lock until explicit same-operation recovery or manual review.

Use existing admin cream/black/orange styling, compact left-aligned controls, visible validation and a review panel showing effective date, unchanged renewal date, regular new monthly price and Stripe charge/billing-credit estimate. Only approved owners can execute financial changes. Conflicting freezes, cancellations, pending invoices, external schedules, incompatible billing modes or unverified prices fail closed with an actionable message.

Verification: unit tests for validation and Stripe request contracts; disposable PostgreSQL integration tests for credit/payment effects and event ordering; UI tests; typecheck, lint, full tests and build; local synthetic browser preview. Do not execute real customer charges or send real emails during verification.
